Current and Completed Projects

Derivative Written Works ("fanfiction", all related to Cyberpunk 2077)

  • I Shoot Holes in the Sun (completed): My first Cyberpunk work, following a young mercenary and her adoptive father's crew as they rise through the ranks of Night City; despite her traumatic past, she learns how to love and live for something other than herself.
  • From the Ashes (in progress): A young feral girl is left for dead in the middle of the desert; she's found by a nomad crew, who nurse her back to health and try to help her heal and navigate a world she's never known: family.
  • Daemons and Deities (on hiatus): A young father with influence and political aspirations has a dream of a better life for his children in Night City; a corrupt megacorporation has other plans. After he is murdered in the streets, his teenage daughter is left with nowhere to go as she tries to protect her younger brother from the cruel reality of life in the under-City.

Original Written Works (all in-progress/planning)
